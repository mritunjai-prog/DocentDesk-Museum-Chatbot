## 📝 PRESENTATION TIPS

### Timing Breakdown:

- **Scene 1 (Welcome)**: 45 seconds
- **Scene 2 (Languages)**: 30 seconds
- **Scene 3 (Themes)**: 15 seconds
- **Scene 4 (Login)**: 20 seconds
- **Scene 5 (Dashboard)**: 40 seconds
- **Scene 6 (Virtual Tour)**: 40 seconds
- **Scene 7 (AI Chatbot)**: 40 seconds
- **Scene 8 (Closing)**: 10 seconds
- **Total**: ~3:40 minutes

### Recording Tips:

1. **Pace**: Speak clearly and confidently, not too fast
2. **Pauses**: Pause 1-2 seconds when switching screens
3. **Mouse**: Move cursor smoothly to highlight features
4. **Emphasis**: Stress key words: "multilingual," "AI-powered," "immersive"
5. **Energy**: Maintain enthusiasm throughout

### Technical Setup:

- **Screen Resolution**: 1920x1080 for best quality
- **Browser Zoom**: 100% (or 90% if UI is too large)
- **Microphone**: Use quality external mic if possible
- **Background**: Quiet environment, no distractions
- **Preparation**: Practice 2-3 times before final recording
